# Shipping-label image attribution

Images were obtained from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-10 as Wikimedia's official 1280-pixel thumbnails. Their descriptions and license declarations are saved in `sources.json`; original URLs, authors and original dimensions are retained there. The four files are independent of the SmartDoc 2015 footage.

- **small-parcel.jpg** — [Kleines paket.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kleines_paket.JPG), Klaus Mueller, 2010-08-17, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). An actual GLS parcel photographed on a table. Original dimensions 2000 × 1537; Wikimedia thumbnail 1280 × 984.
- **package-label-reuse.jpg** — [Package label reuse.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Package_label_reuse.jpg), Klaus Mueller, 2019-03-25, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). A parcel surface with a reuse sticker and a cropped shipping label. Original dimensions 4160 × 3120; Wikimedia thumbnail 1280 × 960.
- **bell-mailing-labels.jpg** — [AT&T Bell Laboratories package mailing labels.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AT%26T_Bell_Laboratories_package_mailing_labels.jpg), Jonathan Schilling, 2020-01-17, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). A blank historical mailing-label pad photographed on wood. Original dimensions 4032 × 3024; Wikimedia thumbnail 1280 × 960. Geometric control with blank shipment fields.
- **korea-parcel-label.jpg** — [Korea stamp for parcel(made by contract logistics).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Korea_stamp_for_parcel(made_by_contract_logistics).jpg), Centrair / Mykim5902, 2019-10-08, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). A close, nearly front-on reproduction of a Korean parcel label, with personal fields already obscured by its publisher. The source metadata lists “own work”; acquisition hardware is unspecified. Original dimensions 2617 × 1810; Wikimedia thumbnail 1280 × 885. Separate masked/cropped-label control.

The corresponding `*-rectified.jpg` files are perspective-corrected derivatives encoded as JPEG quality 95. Geometry overlays draw a red outline over the thumbnail. Each image and its derivatives retain the source image's CC BY-SA version. Code and measurement JSON are separate research artifacts.
